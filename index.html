How to shoot calibration pairs
  1. Best: shoot one RAW, then use the camera's RAW Conversion to save one JPEG as PROVIA and another as the sim you want to calibrate. The two files line up perfectly.
  2. Also good: use Film Simulation Bracketing (BKT), which saves 3 sims from a single exposure.
  3. Otherwise, use a tripod and the same exposure for both shots.
  4. Keep everything else identical: DR100, Highlight/Shadow/Color 0, grain and Color Chrome off, the same white balance (not Auto).
  5. Pick colourful scenes with a wide tonal range (skin, foliage, sky, saturated objects). Each new pair adds to that sim's calibration.

Paste recipes

Paste one or many recipes in the usual "Setting: value" format (from a website, a caption, your notes). Put the recipe's name on its own line above its settings, and any link next to it so the source is credited. Names follow SIM-TempContrast Descriptor: Temp is Warm, Neutral or Cool, and Contrast is Soft, Medium or Hard. Example: CN-WH Portra 400.
